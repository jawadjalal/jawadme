# Loader B words

Seven real Arabic words, written without vowel marks, each drawn as one shaped run (never as separate letters). They stream up through the embers, hang, then dissolve into the mark. Jawad: please check the meanings before this ships.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | Why it is here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| جمر | jamr | embers, live coals | the loader's own material |
| نور | nūr | light | what the embers give |
| خط | khaṭṭ | line, stroke; also calligraphy | the mark is lettering; "khatt" is the word for the art of writing |
| صنع | ṣunʿ | making, crafting | to make something |
| حرفة | ḥirfa | craft, trade | a craft practised by hand |
| بناء | bināʾ | building, construction | he designs and builds things |
| تصميم | taṣmīm | design | the last word to arrive, and the one the mark means |

First visit streams all seven in this order. A repeat visit shows three: جمر، خط، تصميم.
