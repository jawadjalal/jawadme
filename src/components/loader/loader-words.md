# Loader B words

Four real Arabic words, written without vowel marks, each drawn as one shaped run (never as separate letters). They arrive one or two at a time, then تصميم comes apart into embers that gather into the name. Jawad: please check the meanings before this ships.

| Arabic | Transliteration | English | Why it is here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| جمر | jamr | embers, live coals | the loader's own material |
| نور | nūr | light | what the embers give |
| خط | khaṭṭ | line, stroke; also calligraphy | the mark is lettering; "khatt" is also the word for the art of writing |
| تصميم | taṣmīm | design | the last word to arrive, and the one the mark means |

First visit shows all four in this order: جمر، نور، خط, a beat of black, then تصميم. A repeat visit shows جمر, a beat of black, then تصميم.
